Best Coworking Spaces in Bangkok for Digital Nomads 2026

Bangkok remains one of the most popular digital nomad destinations on the planet — and for good reason. The food is exceptional, the cost of living is manageable, and the coworking scene has matured into something genuinely world-class. As of 2026, Bangkok offers over 60 dedicated coworking spaces spread across distinctly different neighborhoods, each with its own personality, pricing tier, and community vibe.

Whether you're a bootstrapped freelancer counting every baht or a six-figure remote engineer looking for premium amenities, there is a space in Bangkok designed for how you work. This guide breaks down the specific spaces you need to know — real names, real prices in THB, and honest neighborhood comparisons.

1. The Hive Thonglor (Piman 49)

Location: Piman 49, Sukhumvit Soi 49, Wattana, Bangkok 10110
BTS: Thonglor (E6) — 10-minute walk or short motorbike taxi
Neighborhood: Thonglor — Bangkok's trendiest district for expats, creatives, and Japanese restaurants

The Hive is one of Bangkok's most established coworking brands, and its Piman 49 flagship occupies a full 7 floors with a mix of hot desks, dedicated desks, private offices, and a stunning rooftop café lounge. The space is designed with a modern industrial aesthetic — exposed concrete, living walls, and floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the space with natural light.

Pricing (2026 Rates)

  • Hot Desk: from THB 4,000/month (~$115 USD)
  • Dedicated Desk: from THB 6,000/month (~$170 USD)
  • Private Office: from THB 9,000/person/month (~$255 USD)
  • Day Pass: THB 350
  • Virtual Office: Enquire directly — varies by package

Amenities

  • Gigabit fiber internet (redundant connections)
  • Rooftop café lounge with barista coffee included
  • Meeting rooms with video conferencing equipment
  • Phone booths for private calls
  • Lockers for day storage
  • Printing and scanning services
  • 24/7 access for monthly members
  • Cleaning service included
  • Community events: weekly networking, skill-sharing workshops, Friday socials

Community Vibe

The Hive skews toward creative professionals, startup founders, and international freelancers. The community is active and social — expect to be greeted at reception and invited to events. The rooftop café is a genuine social hub where members naturally congregate. If you value community connection as much as workspace quality, The Hive delivers.

Multi-Location Access

One standout benefit: Hive membership grants access to sister locations including Common Ground at CentralWorld, Ploenchit, and G Tower, plus The Hive Prakanong. This is invaluable if you need to work in different parts of the city on different days.

2. Hubba-Thonglor

Location: 72 Courtyard, Thonglor Soi 13, Bangkok 10110
BTS: Thonglor (E6) — 15-minute walk or 5-minute taxi
Neighborhood: Thonglor — established expat hub with exceptional dining and nightlife

Hubba is Bangkok's original coworking pioneer, having opened well over a decade ago. It occupies the 72 Courtyard complex — a repurposed commercial building that now houses cafes, restaurants, and creative agency offices. Hubba has a distinctly different energy from newer, more polished spaces: it feels like a creative collective, not a corporate office.

Pricing (2026 Estimates)

  • Hot Desk: THB 3,000—5,000/month (~$85—140 USD)
  • Dedicated Desk: THB 5,500—7,000/month (~$155—200 USD)
  • Private Office: THB 8,000—12,000/person/month (~$225—340 USD)
  • Day Pass: THB 250—300

Amenities

  • Fast fiber internet (up to 500 Mbps)
  • Open-plan coworking hall with high ceilings
  • Private meeting rooms (bookable by the hour)
  • Event space for workshops and meetups
  • On-site cafe with affordable Thai food and coffee
  • Bicycle parking
  • Printer and scanner
  • After-hours access for monthly members

Community Vibe

Hubba's community is its strongest asset. The member base is dominated by digital nomads, solo entrepreneurs, and creative freelancers — designers, writers, photographers, and video editors. The atmosphere is collaborative rather than corporate. Regular events include pitch nights, design critiques, and casual Friday drinks at the courtyard. If you're looking to build a network in Bangkok's creative scene, Hubba is the place.

Neighborhood Notes

Thonglor is one of Bangkok's most desirable neighborhoods, but it's also one of the most expensive for accommodation. Expect to pay THB 15,000—30,000/month for a decent studio or one-bedroom condo within walking distance. The upside: exceptional Japanese restaurants, craft cocktail bars, weekend farmers markets, and the best indie cafes in the city.

3. Glowfish Sathorn

Location: Sathorn Square Office Tower, 98 North Sathorn Road, Silom, Bangkok 10500
BTS: Chong Nonsi (S3) — direct walkway connection
Neighborhood: Sathorn / Silom — Bangkok's central business district

Glowfish is the premium option in this list. Located on a high floor of Sathorn Square, it offers panoramic views of the Chao Phraya River and the Bangkok skyline. This is a workspace designed for professionals who need a quiet, executive-level environment — not a social scene.

Pricing (2026 Estimates)

  • Hot Desk: THB 6,000—9,000/month (~$170—255 USD)
  • Dedicated Desk: THB 9,000—12,000/month (~$255—340 USD)
  • Private Office: THB 15,000—22,000/person/month (~$425—625 USD)
  • Day Pass: THB 500—600

Amenities

  • Enterprise-grade fiber internet with failover (1 Gbps)
  • Floor-to-ceiling windows with river and city views
  • Herman Miller ergonomic chairs
  • Soundproof phone booths (critical for remote workers on client calls)
  • Private meeting rooms with 4K displays and Polycom conferencing
  • Premium coffee bar (barista-made, included in membership)
  • Complimentary snacks and fruit
  • On-site gym partnership
  • 24/7 keycard access
  • Professional mailing address and mail handling

Community Vibe

Glowfish is the quietest, most professional space on this list. The members are primarily senior professionals, C-suite executives running regional operations, and remote workers earning US or European salaries who need a distraction-free environment. Networking here happens organically over coffee, not through organized events. This is the space you choose when you need to get work done — not when you want to socialize.

Neighborhood Notes

Sathorn is Bangkok's financial district. It's quieter on weekends, well-connected by BTS and MRT, and has excellent lunch options in the Sathorn Square food court and adjacent Silom Soi 20 night market. Accommodation nearby (Sathorn, Silom, Chong Nonsi) ranges from THB 10,000—25,000/month for condos. The area is more residential and business-focused than Thonglor, which may appeal to digital nomads seeking a calmer base.

4. Punspace Thonglor

Location: 59/3 Soi Promsri, Sukhumvit Soi 39, Wattana, Bangkok 10110
BTS: Phrom Phong (E5) — 10-minute walk
Neighborhood: Phrom Phong — upscale, family-friendly, with EmQuartier and Emporium malls

Punspace is a well-known Thai coworking chain with locations in Bangkok and Chiang Mai. The Thonglor-adjacent Phrom Phong location is popular among digital nomads who want a balance of community and productivity. The space is designed with a clean, minimalist Scandinavian aesthetic.

Pricing (2026 Estimates)

  • Hot Desk: THB 3,500—4,500/month (~$100—130 USD)
  • Dedicated Desk: THB 5,500—7,000/month (~$155—200 USD)
  • Private Office: Contact for pricing
  • Day Pass: THB 250—300

Amenities

  • Reliable fiber internet (300+ Mbps)
  • Outdoor garden terrace
  • Yoga and meditation sessions (weekly)
  • Nap pods
  • Community kitchen with free tea and coffee
  • Bookable meeting rooms
  • Social events: board game nights, skill-sharing, Friday drinks

Community Vibe

Punspace is beloved by the long-term nomad community for its strong emphasis on work-life balance. The weekly yoga sessions and garden terrace make it easier to maintain well-being while working remotely. The member base is predominantly international, with a healthy mix of European, North American, and Asian digital nomads. The Chiang Mai location (Nimman) means you can split your time between Bangkok and the north seamlessly.

5. Common Ground (CentralWorld & Ploenchit)

Location: CentralWorld (Rama I Road) and Ploenchit BTS area
BTS: Chit Lom (E1) or Ploenchit (E2)
Neighborhood: Central Bangkok — shopping, dining, everything you need within walking distance

Common Ground operates several locations in central Bangkok, often inside or adjacent to major shopping centers. As a member of The Hive network, Common Ground offers a more modern, branded experience with consistent quality across locations.

Pricing (2026 Estimates)

  • Hot Desk: THB 4,500—6,000/month (~$130—170 USD)
  • Dedicated Desk: THB 6,500—8,500/month (~$185—240 USD)
  • Day Pass: THB 350—450

Amenities

  • High-speed fiber internet
  • Professional meeting rooms with AV equipment
  • Event space for networking and workshops
  • Barista coffee bar
  • Business address and mail service
  • Cleaning and maintenance included

Community Vibe

Common Ground attracts a corporate-friendly crowd — think startup teams, agency employees, and independent consultants. The vibe is professional but not stuffy. The CentralWorld location is particularly convenient if you need to shop, eat, or run errands during breaks. It's less intimate than Hubba or Punspace but more polished and consistent.

Cost of Living in Bangkok for Digital Nomads (2026)

Bangkok offers excellent value for digital nomads, but prices have risen post-pandemic. Here's a realistic monthly budget breakdown:

  • Coworking membership: THB 3,000—9,000/month
  • One-bedroom condo: THB 10,000—30,000/month
  • Food (eating out mostly): THB 8,000—15,000/month
  • Transport (BTS/MRT): THB 1,500—4,000/month
  • Gym membership: THB 1,500—3,000/month
  • Utilities & phone: THB 2,000—3,500/month
  • Total estimated monthly cost: THB 26,000—64,500 (~$740—$1,830 USD)

Most digital nomads in Bangkok find they live very comfortably on $1,200—$1,800 USD per month, including coworking, a nice condo, eating out regularly, and social activities. This is roughly half the cost of living in Chiang Mai but still dramatically cheaper than any major Western city.

Pro Tips for Choosing a Bangkok Coworking Space

  1. Try before you commit. Every space on this list offers day passes or free trial days. Spend at least 2—3 days in a space before signing a monthly contract. Internet speed, noise levels, and community fit are hard to judge from a tour.
  2. Check the neighborhood traffic. A coworking space that is a 10-minute BTS ride away might still take 40 minutes by taxi during rush hour. Factor BTS/MRT proximity into your decision.
  3. Ask about events. If community matters to you, ask how many events the space runs per month and what type. Some spaces (Hubba) run 8—12 events monthly. Others (Glowfish) run 0—2.
  4. Consider multi-location access. The Hive and Common Ground networks allow access across multiple Bangkok locations. If you move around the city, this is a significant value-add.
  5. Verify the air quality. Bangkok's PM2.5 levels spike during the dry season (November—June). Choose a space with good air filtration, especially if you plan to stay long-term.
  6. Negotiate for longer stays. Many Bangkok coworking spaces offer discounts for 3-month or 6-month commitments. Always ask — especially if you're staying for a full season.

Honorable Mentions

  • WeWork Sathorn: THB 9,000+/month. Global brand consistency, premium Sathorn location, great for corporate nomads who need an international network.
  • A-Space: THB 3,000—5,000/month. Budget-friendly option with multiple locations. Basic amenities but reliable internet. Popular with Thai freelancers and local startups.
  • True Digital Park: THB 5,000+/month. Massive co-innovation hub near Punnawithi BTS on Sukhumvit Line extension. Startup ecosystem, event space, and cafe. Worth the extra BTS ride.

Final Verdict

Bangkok's coworking scene in 2026 is mature, diverse, and genuinely competitive. For the social nomad who wants community and networking, Hubba and The Hive are the top choices. For the high-earning professional who needs absolute focus, Glowfish is unmatched. For the balanced nomad who values wellness and community equally, Punspace is the sweet spot. And for the budget-conscious, combining a lower-cost neighborhood like Ratchada with a basic coworking membership or cafe-hopping strategy can keep monthly workspace costs under THB 3,000.

Whatever your style, Bangkok has a space that fits. The real challenge isn't finding a place to work — it's choosing between all the incredible food options within walking distance of whichever one you pick.
