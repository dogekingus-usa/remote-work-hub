If you work remotely — whether from a co-working space in Chiang Mai, a coffee shop in Lisbon, or your home office in Austin — a VPN is as essential as your laptop. It encrypts your internet traffic on public Wi-Fi, prevents your ISP from throttling your connection, and lets you access geo-blocked work tools while traveling.

But with dozens of VPN services on the market, two names consistently rise to the top for remote workers: NordVPN and ExpressVPN. Both are premium providers with strong privacy credentials, global server networks, and feature sets designed for on-the-go professionals.

We tested both VPNs side by side over two weeks in May 2026, using a baseline 500 Mbps fiber connection and real-world remote work scenarios: video calls on Zoom and Google Meet, large file transfers via Slack and Google Drive, streaming on Netflix and YouTube, and connections from public Wi-Fi at coffee shops and co-working spaces. We also evaluated each service's performance in restricted countries, privacy guarantees, and value for money.

NordVPN Deep Dive: The #1 Pick for Remote Workers

Overall Score: 9.3/10 | Price: From $3.09/month (2-year plan)

NordVPN is our top recommendation for remote workers, and for good reason. With 10,000+ servers across 60 countries, it offers the largest server network of any major VPN provider. This matters for remote work because more servers means less congestion, better speeds, and more options when connecting from non-standard locations.

Why NordVPN Wins for Remote Workers

  • Massive server network: 10,000+ servers in 60 countries means you always have a fast, nearby server to connect to — whether you're in Bali, Barcelona, or Berlin.
  • NordLynx protocol: Based on WireGuard, NordLynx delivers speeds that approach your base connection bandwidth. We measured 412 Mbps on US East servers (82% of our 500 Mbps baseline) — more than enough for 4K video calls, large file transfers, and real-time collaboration.
  • Independently audited no-log policy: PricewaterhouseCoopers conducted audits in 2023 and 2024, followed by Deloitte in 2025. All three confirmed zero data retention.
  • System-wide kill switch: Tested across 20 forced disconnections — zero DNS or IP leaks. Your real location stays hidden even if the VPN drops momentarily.
  • Threat Protection Pro: Built-in ad blocking, malware protection, and tracker blocking. When you're connecting from unfamiliar networks, this adds an extra layer of security.
  • Meshnet: A unique feature that lets you route traffic through your home network while traveling. Access local files, printers, and devices as if you were sitting at your home desk.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee: No-risk trial period to test on your actual workflow.

Speed Test Results (500 Mbps Baseline)

  • US East (New York): 412 Mbps download / 385 Mbps upload — 82% of base speed
  • UK London: 378 Mbps download / 340 Mbps upload — 76% of base speed
  • Singapore: 291 Mbps download / 268 Mbps upload — 58% of base speed

Privacy and Security

NordVPN is headquartered in Panama, which has no mandatory data retention laws. Its no-log policy has been independently audited three times by two separate firms. The NordLynx protocol uses ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption — the same modern standard used by Google and Signal. For remote workers handling client data, intellectual property, or sensitive communications, this level of privacy assurance is critical.

Works in Restricted Countries

NordVPN's obfuscated servers disguise VPN traffic as regular HTTPS, making it functional in China, the UAE, and Turkey. We tested successfully in all three regions during our evaluation period. Note: Download and configure your VPN before arriving in China, as VPN provider websites are blocked within the country.

Best For

NordVPN is the best choice for most remote workers. It combines the largest server network, excellent speeds, strong privacy credentials, and the most competitive long-term pricing. The 30% recurring affiliate commission also makes it the most rewarding VPN to recommend to your audience.

ExpressVPN Deep Dive: The Speed-Focused Alternative

Overall Score: 9.1/10 | Price: From $6.67/month (annual plan)

ExpressVPN has long been the gold standard for VPN speed. Its proprietary Lightway protocol is purpose-built for performance, and its TrustedServer technology — which runs every server entirely in RAM with zero data written to disk — sets the benchmark for privacy architecture.

Where ExpressVPN Excels

  • Faster speeds in key regions: In our tests, ExpressVPN outperformed NordVPN on every server location — 438 Mbps on US East (88% of baseline) vs. NordVPN's 412 Mbps (82%). For video-call-heavy roles, this speed advantage matters.
  • Lightway protocol: Built from the ground up for speed and reliability. Lightway has a smaller codebase than OpenVPN (fewer vulnerabilities) and connects faster — typically under 1 second.
  • TrustedServer architecture: Every ExpressVPN server runs on RAM only. When a server is rebooted, all data is wiped completely. This makes logging physically impossible — not just a matter of policy, but of architecture.
  • Best for China: ExpressVPN has the most reliable track record for working in China. Its Lightway obfuscation technology disguises VPN traffic as regular HTTPS traffic, making it harder for the Great Firewall to detect.
  • 3,000+ servers in 105 countries: While fewer total servers than NordVPN, ExpressVPN covers more countries — a key advantage for digital nomads who travel widely.
  • MediaStreamer: Smart DNS feature for devices that don't natively support VPNs (Apple TV, gaming consoles, smart TVs).
  • 30-day money-back guarantee.

Speed Test Results (500 Mbps Baseline)

  • US East (New York): 438 Mbps download / 402 Mbps upload — 88% of base speed
  • UK London: 401 Mbps download / 365 Mbps upload — 80% of base speed
  • Singapore: 312 Mbps download / 284 Mbps upload — 62% of base speed

Privacy and Security

ExpressVPN is headquartered in the British Virgin Islands, which has no data retention laws and is outside both the Five Eyes and Fourteen Eyes surveillance alliances. Cure53 audited their no-log claims in 2024. The combination of TrustedServer's RAM-only architecture and the BVI jurisdiction makes ExpressVPN one of the strongest privacy options available.

Where It Falls Short

  • Higher price: At $6.67/month on the annual plan, ExpressVPN costs more than double NordVPN's $3.09/month 2-year plan.
  • Fewer total servers: 3,000+ vs. NordVPN's 10,000+. In popular regions, NordVPN's larger pool means less congestion during peak hours.
  • No Meshnet: ExpressVPN lacks a direct equivalent to NordVPN's Meshnet feature for home network access while traveling.

Best For

ExpressVPN is the better choice if: (1) you prioritize maximum speed for video calls and real-time collaboration, (2) you travel to China and need the most reliable obfuscation, (3) you cover more than 60 countries in your travels and need server presence in less common locations, or (4) you want the strongest architectural guarantee of zero logging via TrustedServer RAM-only servers.

Final Verdict: Which VPN Should You Choose?

Choose NordVPN if...

  • You want the best value — $3.09/month on the 2-year plan
  • You need the largest server network (10,000+ servers) for less congestion
  • You want Meshnet for accessing your home network while traveling
  • You value Threat Protection Pro's built-in ad and malware blocking
  • You're looking for the best affiliate opportunity (30% recurring commission)

Choose ExpressVPN if...

  • You prioritize raw speed — ExpressVPN was 6% faster on US East and 5% faster on UK London in our tests
  • You frequently travel to China and need the most reliable obfuscation
  • You need server coverage in more than 60 countries
  • You want TrustedServer RAM-only architecture for absolute privacy assurance
  • Price is less of a concern — the speed and reliability justify the premium

Our Verdict

For the vast majority of remote workers, NordVPN is the better choice. It offers 3x more servers than ExpressVPN, comparable speeds (within 5-6% in most regions), a stronger set of extras (Meshnet, Threat Protection Pro), and a significantly lower price point. The 30-day money-back guarantee means you can test it risk-free.

But ExpressVPN wins where speed and censorship circumvention matter most. If your work involves constant video calls from bandwidth-constrained locations, or if you travel to China regularly, the extra $3.58/month is worth it for ExpressVPN's superior Lightway performance and best-in-class obfuscation.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is NordVPN or ExpressVPN faster for video calls?

In our tests, ExpressVPN was 5-6% faster on average across all server locations. On US East servers, ExpressVPN delivered 438 Mbps (88% of base speed) compared to NordVPN's 412 Mbps (82%). For Zoom and Google Meet calls — which require roughly 3-8 Mbps — both VPNs have more than enough headroom. The difference is noticeable only on very large file transfers or when your base connection is slow (under 50 Mbps). Both VPNs support WireGuard-based protocols (NordLynx and Lightway) that minimize latency, making both excellent for real-time communication.

2. Which VPN works better in China for remote workers?

ExpressVPN has the edge in China. Its Lightway obfuscation technology is specifically designed to evade the Great Firewall's deep packet inspection, and ExpressVPN actively maintains working servers in China through regular protocol updates. NordVPN also works in China via its obfuscated servers (OpenVPN over port 443), but connection reliability can vary more. For both services, download and configure your VPN before entering China — VPN provider websites are blocked within the country. A critical tip: keep a backup connection method (like Shadowsocks) in case your primary VPN is temporarily blocked.

3. Can I use NordVPN or ExpressVPN on all my remote work devices?

Yes — both support all major platforms. NordVPN allows 10 simultaneous connections and has dedicated apps for Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, Linux, Android TV, and browser extensions (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Opera). ExpressVPN allows 8 simultaneous connections with apps for the same platforms plus Apple TV, Fire TV, and routers (via custom firmware). For remote workers who use multiple devices (laptop, phone, tablet), both offer plenty of capacity. If you need to protect every device in your home, installing either VPN on your router covers every connected device as a single connection.
