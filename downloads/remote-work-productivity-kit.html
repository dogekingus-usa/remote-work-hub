Daily workflow template (deep-focus optimized)

  1. 09:00-09:30 — Inbox zero + today's top 3 priorities (write them down)
  2. 09:30-11:30 — Deep work block #1 (no notifications, phone in another room)
  3. 11:30-12:00 — Meetings/async check-ins
  4. 12:00-13:00 — Lunch away from the desk
  5. 13:00-15:00 — Deep work block #2 (hardest remaining task first)
  6. 15:00-15:30 — Email + Slack batch response
  7. 15:30-17:00 — Shallow work: admin, planning, tomorrow's list
  8. 17:00 — Shutdown ritual: close tabs, write tomorrow's top 3, log off

Time zone management cheat sheet

  • ☐ Use a world-clock widget (everyone's hours visible at a glance)
  • ☐ Keep a shared "core hours" block (e.g., 4h overlap daily)
  • ☐ Async-first: document decisions so no one waits on a meeting
  • ☐ Schedule meetings in the overlap window, not your morning
  • ☐ Use Loom/Loom-style video for updates across time zones

Home office setup checklist (ergonomic + budget)

  • ☐ Chair: lumbar support, adjustable height (or a lumbar cushion)
  • ☐ Desk at elbow height — feet flat, forearms parallel
  • ☐ Monitor at arm's length, top of screen at eye level
  • ☐ External keyboard + mouse (laptop-only setups wreck posture)
  • ☐ Lighting: natural light front/side, avoid backlight glare
  • ☐ Headset with noise-cancel mic (calls sound professional)
  • ☐ Backup internet: phone hotspot configured
  • ☐ $50 upgrade path: lumbar cushion → monitor riser → second monitor

Start with ONE change today — the shutdown ritual. It pays off immediately in focus and work-life separation.
