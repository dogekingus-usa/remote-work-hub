The Best Digital Nomad Cities for 2026

We analyzed 20+ cities across 4 criteria: cost of living, internet speed, community size, and quality of life. Here are the top 10 ranked.

1. Chiang Mai, Thailand — $800/mo

Still the digital nomad capital. Fast internet (200+ Mbps), huge nomad community, amazing food. 1BR: $300-500, co-working: $100-150/mo, food: $5-10/day.

2. Medellín, Colombia — $1,000/mo

Spring weather year-round, growing tech scene, affordable. 1BR: $400-600, co-working: $150-200/mo. Digital nomad visa available.

3. Lisbon, Portugal — $1,500/mo

European hub with great infrastructure, D7 visa for remote workers. 1BR: $800-1200, co-working: $200-300/mo. Higher cost but excellent quality of life.

4. Bali (Canggu), Indonesia — $900/mo

Surf, work, repeat. Massive nomad community. 1BR: $400-700, co-working: $150-200/mo. Internet improving rapidly (100+ Mbps).

5. Mexico City, Mexico — $1,100/mo

Cultural capital of Latin America. Great food, arts, and nomad scene. 1BR: $500-800, co-working: $150-250/mo. MM visa for remote workers.

6-10: Ho Chi Minh City, Bucharest, Split, Buenos Aires, Cape Town

These cities offer sub-$1,000/mo living with reliable internet and growing nomad communities. Each has unique advantages: Bucharest for EU access, Buenos Aires for culture, Split for summer living.

Pro Tips

Always test internet before renting. Join local nomad Facebook groups for housing. Get a local SIM (eSIM works great). Use coworking spaces for productivity and networking.
