Asia

1. Hive — Bangkok, Thailand

Bangkok has become the unofficial capital of the digital nomad world, and Hive is its crown jewel. With four locations including Thonglor and Ekkamai, Hive offers 24/7 access, gigabit fiber internet, private phone booths, and weekly networking events. Hot desks start at $150 per month, dedicated desks from $250. The rooftop terraces and on-site cafes make it easy to build a network from scratch.

2. Outpost — Ubud, Bali, Indonesia

Bali remains a magnet for remote workers, and Outpost in Ubud sets the gold standard. Surrounded by rice paddies and jungle, it combines coworking with coliving featuring Starlink internet, a saltwater pool, and an on-site restaurant. Hot desks start at $130 per month. Coliving packages from $650 include workspace, accommodation, and breakfast.

3. The Hive — Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Vietnam offers exceptional value for nomads, and The Hive in District 1 delivers quality coworking with soundproof meeting rooms and a buzzing cafe. Monthly hot desks cost approximately $90, making it one of the most affordable high-quality options in Asia. Regular skill-exchange meetups help newcomers settle in fast.

4. Papercup — Tokyo, Japan

Tokyo's coworking scene blends minimalist aesthetics with efficiency, and Papercup in Shibuya captures this perfectly with curated playlists, a Japanese tea bar, and membership capped at 80 people. Hot desks cost around $250 per month. The location near Shibuya Crossing gives you access to Tokyo's best networking events.

Europe

5. Betahaus — Berlin, Germany

Housed in a converted Kreuzberg factory, Betahaus offers high ceilings, exposed brick, a rooftop garden, and creative-community energy. Monthly memberships start at approximately €180. Regular founder talks, design sprints, and summer barbecues build genuine community.

6. Second Home — Lisbon, Portugal

Lisbon's Second Home is a greenhouse-like coworking space surrounded by greenery at the Mercado de Campo de Ourique. Memberships cost around €220 per month and include community lunches, investor office hours, and cultural events. Portugal's D7 visa makes longer stays straightforward.

7. WeWork Sarphatistraat — Amsterdam, Netherlands

Built inside a former newspaper printing house, this WeWork flagship features a wellness room, canal-side terrace, and on-site barista. Hot desks run approximately €250 per month. Amsterdam's central location makes weekend exploration of Europe easy.

8. Soho House — Barcelona, Spain

Housed in a restored 18th-century palace, Barcelona's Soho House pairs a rooftop pool, gym, and cinema with dedicated workspace floors. Coworking memberships cost around €260 per month. The creative member base creates an inspiring environment for deep work.

9. Mindspace — London, United Kingdom

Mindspace near Liverpool Street offers wellness studios, meditation rooms, and complimentary craft beer. Hot desks start at approximately £350 per month. The member roster includes major tech companies and scale-ups for high-level networking.

10. Colab — Dublin, Ireland

Located in Temple Bar, Colab offers affordable coworking at approximately €170 per month with podcast studios and a member booking app. The community leans toward SaaS founders and developers drawn by Ireland's corporate tax environment.

North America

11. NeueHouse — New York City, USA

NeueHouse Madison Square offers premium coworking in a landmark building with a library, screening room, and rooftop terrace. Memberships start at $350 per month. The curated member base includes Pulitzer winners and Fortune 500 consultants.

12. Industrious — Austin, Texas, USA

Austin's Congress Avenue Industrious delivers hospitality-driven coworking with fresh breakfast, craft coffee, and biweekly events. Hot desks cost around $300 per month. Walking distance to Austin's legendary food trucks and Lady Bird Lake.

Latin America & Africa

13. Selina — Medellín, Colombia

Medellín's Selina in El Poblado features a pool, rooftop bar, and co-working garden. Hot desks cost around $100 per month, coliving packages from $500. Spring-like weather year-round and Metro cable cars with mountain views make this a nomad favorite.

14. Workshop 17 — Cape Town, South Africa

Cape Town's V&A Waterfront Workshop 17 offers fibre internet, phone booths, and views of Table Mountain. Memberships cost approximately $140 per month. The city's hiking, wine country, and affordable dining make it an increasingly popular 2026 destination.

FAQ: Coworking Spaces for Digital Nomads in 2026

1. How much should I expect to pay for a coworking space as a digital nomad?

Monthly hot desk prices vary by region. Southeast Asia: $90—$150. Southern Europe: $200—$260. Major US cities: $250—$400. Many spaces offer day passes ($10—$30) and weekly rates. Always check for nomad discounts and monthly membership caps.

2. What should I look for before committing to a coworking space?

Test Wi-Fi speed (minimum 50 Mbps for video calls), check for backup internet, ergonomic seating, and power outlets. Visit during peak hours to assess noise levels. Ask about community events and member demographics. A trial day pass is always recommended before signing a monthly contract.

3. Is it safe to use public Wi-Fi in coworking spaces?

No. Shared Wi-Fi networks are inherently insecure. Even password-protected networks can be compromised. You must use a VPN like NordVPN to encrypt all traffic and protect client data, login credentials, and personal information. Never log into banking or work systems on shared Wi-Fi without active VPN protection.
