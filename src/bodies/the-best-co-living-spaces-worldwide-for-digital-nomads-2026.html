The Best Co-Living Spaces Worldwide for Digital Nomads 2026

Published: May 15, 2026 | Reading time: 4 min

In the ever-evolving world of digital nomadism, choosing the right co-living space can significantly impact your productivity and overall quality of life. For 2026, we've curated a list of some of the best co-living spaces worldwide that cater specifically to digital nomads. These hubs not only offer top-notch amenities but also foster a vibrant community environment perfect for networking, collaboration, and personal growth.

Top Co-Living Spaces in Asia

  • Hive Co-Working Space in Tokyo, Japan: This co-living space offers modern apartments with all the comforts of home. It's strategically located near major tech hubs like Shibuya and Shinjuku. The community is known for its supportive environment, regular social events, and access to high-speed internet.
  • Co-lab in Singapore: Situated in the heart of Singapore's central business district, Co-lab offers a perfect blend of comfort and convenience. It features fully equipped private rooms and shared spaces where residents can meet new people or collaborate on projects. The space also includes a rooftop garden that's ideal for relaxation and socializing.
  • Regus in Manila, Philippines: Regus is a well-established co-working brand with locations across the globe, including Manila. It offers various accommodation options from private rooms to full apartments. What sets it apart are its flexible membership plans, which allow you to work where and when you need.

Europe's Best Co-Living Spaces for Digital Nomads

  • The Nest in Berlin, Germany: This co-living space is a favorite among digital nomads due to its vibrant community atmosphere. It's located in the trendy Kreuzberg district and offers both private rooms and shared spaces. The Nest also hosts regular events such as workshops, meetups, and cultural activities that help residents connect with one another.
  • Beagle House in Lisbon, Portugal: For those who appreciate a slower pace of life combined with excellent tech infrastructure, Beagle House is the perfect choice. Situated just outside the city center, it offers cozy, spacious rooms with modern amenities and fast internet connections. The house hosts regular events like language exchanges and cooking classes to help residents bond.
  • Basecamp in Barcelona, Spain: Located in the Gothic Quarter, Basecamp provides a unique living experience for digital nomads. It's a blend of co-working space and private accommodation with modern facilities. The community is known for its inclusivity and frequent social gatherings that include movie nights, karaoke, and live music events.

Americas' Leading Co-Living Spaces for Digital Nomads

  • Home Base in New York City, USA: For those based in or visiting NYC, Home Base offers stylish apartments with all the amenities you need. Its prime location makes it easy to commute to major tech companies and startup incubators. The community is highly diverse, offering a rich mix of cultures and backgrounds.
  • Cowork.com in San Francisco, USA: Located in the heart of Silicon Valley, this co-living space is ideal for tech-savvy digital nomads. It combines state-of-the-art co-working facilities with private rooms, providing both flexibility and convenience. Regular networking events and workshops help residents stay connected and informed about the latest trends.
  • La Casa in Mexico City, Mexico: This co-living space offers a blend of modern living spaces and traditional Mexican culture. Situated in one of the city's up-and-coming neighborhoods, it provides easy access to vibrant markets, trendy cafes, and cultural events. The community is known for its warm hospitality and frequent social gatherings.

Why Choose These Co-Living Spaces?

  • Community Building: Each of these spaces fosters a strong sense of community through regular meetups, workshops, and cultural events. This helps digital nomads form lasting connections with like-minded individuals.
  • Work-Life Balance: Many co-living spaces offer both private rooms for focused work and shared spaces for relaxation or socializing. This balance is crucial for maintaining mental well-being while working remotely.
  • Location and Amenities: These spaces are strategically located in bustling tech hubs, ensuring residents have easy access to resources and opportunities. They also come equipped with modern amenities such as high-speed internet, on-site gyms, and cafes.

Conclusion

Selecting the right co-living space can make a significant difference in your digital nomad journey. Whether you're looking for a vibrant community in Asia, a blend of culture and tech in Europe, or the energy of Silicon Valley in America, these top co-living spaces offer everything you need to thrive as a remote worker. By choosing one of these destinations, you can ensure that your travel and work experiences are both productive and enriching.
