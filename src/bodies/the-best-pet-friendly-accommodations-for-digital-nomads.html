The Best Pet Friendly Accommodations for Digital Nomads

Whether you're a digital nomad or someone who travels frequently with your furry friend, finding the right pet-friendly accommodations is essential. Here are some of the best options to ensure both you and your pets have a comfortable stay.

Pet-Friendly Hotels

For those who prefer the convenience of hotels, there are many pet-friendly options available. Here are some of the best:

  • Marriott International: Marriott has a growing list of pet-friendly properties worldwide, offering amenities like pet beds and even doggy daycare services in select locations.
  • Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts: With its commitment to sustainability and comfort, Four Seasons often welcomes pets with open arms. Check their website for specific locations that allow pets.
  • Wyndham Hotels & Resorts: Wyndham offers pet-friendly options in over 35 countries, providing a comfortable stay for both humans and pets.

Pet-Friendly Apartments and Condos

Apartments and condos can be ideal for digital nomads as they often offer more space. Here are some recommendations:

  • Long-Term Rentals on Airbnb: Airbnb hosts often welcome pets, making long-term stays possible. Ensure to check their pet policies before booking.
  • Vrbo (Vacation Rentals by Owner): Similar to Airbnb, Vrbo offers a wide range of vacation rentals that may accommodate pets. Be sure to read the fine print regarding pet fees and restrictions.

Cozy B&Bs

B&Bs can offer a charming and personalized experience for both you and your pet:

  • Pet-Friendly B&Bs in the USA (e.g., Dog House Bed & Breakfast): Many boutique bed and breakfast establishments welcome pets, providing extra amenities like treats and dog beds.
  • International Pet-Friendly B&Bs: Look for listings on sites like Tripadvisor or Google Maps that indicate pet-friendly accommodations in your travel destination.

Vacation Rentals with a Pet-Friendly Twist

Vacation rentals can be particularly appealing if you want to have more control over the living space and amenities:

  • Rentals on Airbnb: Many Airbnb hosts offer pet-friendly listings, often including pet beds, toys, and even doggy doors.
  • Vacation Rentals on HomeAway: This platform has a wide range of properties that are pet-friendly, offering everything from single rooms to entire houses with outdoor spaces for pets to play in.

Additional Tips for Traveling with Pets

To ensure a smooth experience when traveling with your furry friend:

  • Check Local Laws and Regulations: Each country or city may have different rules regarding pets, so it's important to research beforehand.
  • Pack Pet Essentials: Bring along food, medications, toys, and any other essentials your pet might need during the trip.
  • Book with Advance Notice: Always book accommodations as early as possible, especially in popular tourist destinations or peak travel seasons.
